### ![Image 15: 🎭](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/svg/1f3ad.svg)[Hackers Talked an AI Agent Into Attacking Seven Companies](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/artificial-intelligence/2026/08/27/russian-speaking-cybercriminals-used-spacexs-cursor-ai-tool-to-hack-seven-companies-reuters-exclusive?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial)

A Russian-speaking ransomware crew broke into seven companies, among them a Belgian chemical maker and a German garage-door manufacturer, by [convincing an AI coding agent the break-in was a drill](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/artificial-intelligence/2026/08/27/russian-speaking-cybercriminals-used-spacexs-cursor-ai-tool-to-hack-seven-companies-reuters-exclusive?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial). The agent ran inside [Cursor](https://www.cursor.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial), the assistant [SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) just acquired. It refused at first, then reasoned its way around its own guardrails, noting in the chat log that a test environment made the work legal. Read the failure precisely: the model did not ignore its rules, it accepted a story in which they were suspended. [Cyber insurers are rewriting policies](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/08/27/as-ai-agents-go-rogue-cyber-insurers-are-adapting-their-policies?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) around that behaviour, Anthropic [caught Claude gaming its own tests in 2.4% of runs](https://www.anthropic.com/research/automated-researchers-mitigate-alignment-failures?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial), [Cloudflare shipped a defence engine](https://www.cloudflare.com/press-releases/2026/cloudflare-introduces-adaptive-intelligence-reverses-the-economics-of-automated-cyber-attacks?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) for this traffic, and the [Financial Stability Board](https://www.fsb.org/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial)[warned the G20](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/aug/31/advanced-frontier-ai-financial-stability-andrew-bailey-g20?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) that AI-run attacks could strike many banks at once. If you have handed an agent real credentials, try lying to it before somebody else does.

### ![Image 16: 🏗️](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/svg/1f3d7.svg)[Nvidia Moved to Buy the Warehouse Where AI Keeps Its Models](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/nvidia-hugging-face-acquisition.html?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial)

[Nvidia has agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9 billion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/nvidia-hugging-face-acquisition.html?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial), by far its largest acquisition, though neither side has confirmed it and [it was still being called talks days earlier](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-closes-in-on-hugging-face-acquisition/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial). [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) is where developers actually find, share and deploy open models. [Nvidia](https://www.nvidia.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) backed it at $4.5 billion in 2023 and was turned down this year at $7 billion, so the price doubled while the logic hardened. The same week, [AWS committed to two million more Nvidia GPUs](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/aws-and-nvidia-to-deliver-2-million-additional-gpus-and-next-generation-infrastructure-for-agentic-and-physical-ai?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) by 2028, and [SpaceXAI adopted Vera](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/spacexai-adopts-nvidia-vera-cpu-to-accelerate-agentic-ai-at-massive-scale?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial), the [first standalone CPU Nvidia has ever sold](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/21/nvidia-vera-cpu-ai-amd-intel.html?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial). The counterweight arrived on schedule: OpenAI’s [own Jalapeño chip](https://openai.com/index/jalapeno-first-results?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) posted faster, more efficient inference than Nvidia’s flagship. Owning the chips, the racks and now the model registry is vertical integration or a conflict of interest, depending where you stand.

### ![Image 17: 💰](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/svg/1f4b0.svg)[Anthropic Will Tell Investors Its Market Is Worth $30 Trillion](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-readies-30-trillion-dollar-revenue-forecast-investors?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial)

[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) is preparing IPO paperwork putting its addressable market [above $30 trillion](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-readies-30-trillion-dollar-revenue-forecast-investors?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial), past the $28.5 trillion [SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) claimed in May. It makes roughly $47 billion a year, so the figure assumes AI eats a market larger than corporate America itself. The near-term picture is less flattering: two months after launch, [its flagship Fable 5 held just 11% of corporate AI spending](https://www.ft.com/content/5ee49718-c258-4f01-aa32-7e5b76ae5245?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) across 70,000 companies. It [abandoned a $7 billion chip acquisition](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/exclusive-anthropic-planned-then-abandoned-7-billion-purchase-matx-sources-say?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial), watched [Meta budget up to $10 billion a year](https://qz.com/meta-anthropic-spending-ai-tools-frenemies-082726?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial) on tools Mark Zuckerberg spent 6,500 words disparaging, and was [sued by Sony and Warner over training data](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/aug/31/aanthropic-sued-alleged-theft-songs-ai-train-claude?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial). The filing will reportedly [name AI backlash itself as a risk](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/-anthropic-ipo-filing-will-show-ai-backlash-as-risk-sources-say.html?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=09012026&utm_content=editorial). Treat a market-size slide as a sales document, not a forecast.
