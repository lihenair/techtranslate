### ![Image 15: 🏦](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/svg/1f3e6.svg)[Nvidia Became OpenAI’s Banker, and Anthropic Headed for the Exit](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial)

The money in AI stopped buying chips and started underwriting them. Nvidia agreed to guarantee roughly $105 billion behind a 10-gigawatt campus in Pike County, Ohio, on a decommissioned uranium enrichment site, leased for 20 years by [OpenAI](https://openai.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial) and built by [SB Energy](https://sbenergy.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial). It is the largest such project ever announced. Nvidia already holds about $30 billion of OpenAI equity, making it supplier, lender and landlord to its own biggest customer, and it then spent [$6 billion licensing Poolside](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/nvidia-is-spending-6-billion-to-build-a-powerful-u-s-alternative-to-chinese-ai-c51c38cc?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial). Meanwhile [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial) told investors an IPO could [raise over $100 billion near a $2 trillion valuation](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/-anthropic-ipo-filing-will-show-ai-backlash-as-risk-sources-say.html?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial), after already [passing OpenAI on quarterly revenue](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/stock-winners-and-losers-as-anthropic-passes-openai-as-hottest-ai-upstart.html?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial). [Alibaba and Tencent spent $18 billion in one quarter](https://en.sedaily.com/international/2026/08/23/alibaba-tencent-pour-18-billion-into-ai-in-second-quarter?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial). When the vendor holds the mortgage, a demand shortfall stops being the customer’s problem.

### ![Image 16: 🧬](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/svg/1f9ec.svg)[AI Picked the Targets in a Cancer Drug That Just Passed Phase 3](https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-announce-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-of-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-met-endpoints-of-recurrence-free-survival-rfs-and-distant-metastasis-free-survival-dmfs-in-patient?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial)

The industry has promised for years that AI would help cure cancer. This week it produced a receipt. Merck and [Moderna](https://www.modernatx.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial) reported the first positive Phase 3 result for an individualised mRNA cancer therapy. [Moderna’s algorithms](https://www.modernatx.com/en-US/media-center/all-media/blogs/advancing-fight-against-cancer?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial) read sequencing data from each patient’s own tumour and blood, weigh that cancer’s mutations, and predict up to 34 neoantigens most likely to provoke an immune response. Those targets are encoded into an mRNA therapy made for that one person and given with [Keytruda](https://www.keytruda.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial). In the melanoma trial the combination significantly improved recurrence-free and distant-metastasis-free survival against Keytruda alone, though overall-survival follow-up is still running. Be precise about what happened here: AI did not invent a drug. It did the selection, patient by patient, at a scale no manual review could match.

### ![Image 17: 🔀](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/svg/1f500.svg)[AT&T Sent 40% of Its AI Work to Open Models](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/applied-ai/t-using-open-source-models-curb-anthropic-bills?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial)

[AT&T](https://www.att.com/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial) is routing a growing share of internal AI work to open-weight models it runs itself, escalating only harder jobs to frontier systems. Roughly 40% of employee usage now goes to open models, with open-model coding reportedly cutting costs 56% for about a 2% quality tradeoff, and smart routing cutting some applications 80% to 90%. Independent evidence landed the same week: in [Aikido’s 11.7-billion-token security test](https://www.aikido.dev/blog/ai-model-benchmarks-aug-21-2026?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial), three cheap open-model runs beat a single Opus 5 or Grok pass on coverage. Routing is becoming its own layer, with [Ramp shipping a router](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/ramp-launches-its-own-ai-model-router-called-router?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial), [Callosum raising $100 million](https://www.callosum.com/blog/seed-round?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial), and [Stripe reported to be buying](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial)[OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/?utm_source=aiweekly&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=08252026&utm_content=editorial). The catch is measurement. If you cannot say what good enough looks like on your own work, a router is a roulette wheel with a billing address.
